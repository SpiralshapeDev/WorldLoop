This mod makes it so that when touching the edge of the world border, you enter another world which is the same as your current world but with a different seed!
Discord [ https://discord.com/invite/A7wBSSFfAD ]
Source [ https://https://github.com/SpiralshapeDev/ContinuedWorlds ]

[h1][u] Warning! [/u][/h1]
This mod is not made for long-term playthroughs and may break world files. Remember to keep backups.
This was made because I thought the idea was interesting & will likely not be receiving future updates else than small changes & bugfixes.
